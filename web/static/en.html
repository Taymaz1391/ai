How it works

From raw Persian text to an answer

  1. NormaliseArabic letters, digits, diacritics and invisible marks are unified before anything else.
  2. TokeniseA byte-level BPE model with a 10,000-token vocabulary trained only on Persian corpora.
  3. PredictA decoder-only transformer with RoPE-style positions, GQA attention and SwiGLU blocks.
  4. PolishSampling, repetition control and a Persian post-processor shape the final answer.

Details
Honest limits

What this deployment cannot do

Being clear is part of the product. These are the limits of this machine, not marketing.

1 billion concurrent users

Impossible on one CPU node. The architecture doc explains how replicas, a queue and a CDN would scale it; the sandbox runs a single process.

Trillion-parameter training

A billion-parameter run needs GPUs, days of compute and a curated corpus. This repository trains a 6.5M model on 2 vCPUs on purpose.

"Train on all of Google"

Crawling and training on that corpus is neither legal nor feasible here. Rayan uses public Persian corpora with recorded licences.

ChatGPT-level English

The checkpoint only knows Persian. The English interface is real, the English answers are not — and we will not pretend otherwise.
