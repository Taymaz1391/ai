The five-hour course

Ten lessons, 300 minutes, from a raw Persian corpus to a product that runs on the internet. Lessons, labs and the final exam are written in Persian — the language the model speaks — and every command points at a file in this repository. This page is the English map of the curriculum.

Module 0 · Getting started 45 min

What “from scratch” means, what this model can and cannot do, and how to get the repo running locally.

Lesson 1 · 20 min

What a language model really is

Next-token prediction, parameters, loss and perplexity. You will read the real numbers of the checkpoint (6,574,848 parameters, validation loss 5.3049) and compute perplexity by hand.

Open lesson 1 (Persian)

Lesson 2 · 25 min

Environment and first run

Clone, virtualenv, install, boot the server, run both test suites. A reproducible environment is the first product feature.

Open lesson 2 (Persian)

Module 1 · Data and tokens 60 min

Quality before architecture: clean the corpus, then turn Persian text into integers.

Lesson 3 · 30 min

The Persian corpus

News, classic poetry and a small dialogue set; normalisation (Arabic yeh/kaf, ZWNJ, digits), deduplication and a leak-free validation split. Licences live in NOTICE.md.

Open lesson 3 (Persian)

Lesson 4 · 30 min

A BPE tokeniser for Persian

Why characters and whole words both fail, how merge order works, and what a 10,000-token vocabulary buys you in compression and cost.

Open lesson 4 (Persian)

Module 2 · The model 70 min

A small transformer that runs on NumPy alone and trains on two CPU cores.

Lesson 5 · 35 min

Transformer, from zero

Embeddings, positional signals, multi-head attention, the causal mask, MLP, residual paths and the KV cache. Every tensor shape is written down, and the parameter table adds up to 6,574,848.

Open lesson 5 (Persian)

Lesson 6 · 35 min

Training and optimisation

Cross-entropy, AdamW, warmup, clipping, batch size, 2,400 steps and 14.7M tokens seen. How to read a loss curve without fooling yourself.

Open lesson 6 (Persian)

Module 3 · The product 65 min

A model without a product is just a file. Turn it into something people use.

Lesson 7 · 30 min

Inference, streaming and the API

Temperature, top-k and top-p; NDJSON streaming; deterministic tools for arithmetic; the concurrency semaphore and why a queue beats a crash.

Open lesson 7 (Persian)

Lesson 8 · 35 min

Interface, PWA and two languages

Design tokens, RTL, self-hosted fonts, dark mode, service-worker caching and the locale decision that picks a language and a video cut for each visitor.

Open lesson 8 (Persian)

Module 4 · Security and deployment 60 min

Boundaries and capacity, stated with numbers.

Lesson 9 · 30 min

Security, quotas and the admin boundary

scrypt password hashing, CSRF, login lockout, quota accounting, CSP headers, and the closed surfaces: model weights and the self-test answer only to an admin session.

Open lesson 9 (Persian)

Lesson 10 · 30 min

Deployment, measurement, scaling

Docker, Render and Hugging Face Spaces; the honest numbers (about 84 tokens/s and 0.3–0.4 requests/s on two cores) and the three real steps toward more traffic.

Open lesson 10 (Persian)

After the course

Build one thing end to end

Pick one: a two-layer miniature model, a quality improvement under the same compute budget, or a new bilingual page that follows every rule of this repository. Deliver a short README: what you built, how you measured it, and what did not work. The third part matters most.
